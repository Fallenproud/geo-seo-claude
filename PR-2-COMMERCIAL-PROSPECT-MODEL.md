# PR-2 — Commercial Prospect Model

Canonical acceptance criteria for the commercial CRM hardening stage.

- Identity, geography, industry, acquisition, contacts, target queries, and competitors are represented.
- GEO readiness and observed AI visibility remain separate.
- Opportunity value and contracted recurring revenue remain separate.
- Pipeline valuation is deterministic and probability-weighted.
- Legacy records normalize safely.
- Commercial measurements retain provenance.
- Unknown measurements remain null.
- Tests cover normalization, serialization, valuation, and invalid inputs.

Out of scope: live AI query collection, platform integrations, revenue attribution, and SaaS database migration.
